`icon-placeholder.png` and `adaptive-icon-foreground-placeholder.png` are **temporary launcher-icon
placeholders** (a white ring on black) made by `scripts/make-placeholder-icons.py`, which also writes
the PWA icons in `public/icons/`.

- The wide SOUL wordmark is never squeezed into an app icon. Full logo: splash and branding.
  Compact mark: launcher and app icon (DECISIONS C-21).
- The final compact mark is a **release blocker** until the owner approves an original or
  licensed asset (C-21). The full logo itself is cleared for public use by the owner (C-22).
